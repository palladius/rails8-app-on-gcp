## 🎯 Session Focus: Turning a Rails 8 Workshop into an Antigravity 2.0 Workshop

Building on lessons learned from the premiere delivery at DevFest Modena (Sat 3 Oct 2026), this edition places **Google Antigravity 2.0** front and center as the developer's autonomous pair programmer:

1. **Step 1 — Install Google Antigravity 2.0** (`https://antigravity.google/download` — using **WSL** on Windows).
2. **Step 2 — Open Official Google Codelab** (`#0` introduction & architecture overview).
3. **Step 3 — Sign in with Gmail** (`@gmail.com`).
4. **Step 4 — Prompt 1 (Clone & Chime):** Ask Antigravity to clone `https://github.com/palladius/rails8-app-on-gcp/` into `~/Documents` and play a sound when done.
5. **Step 5 — Open Scoped Project:** Open a new project inside `Documents/rails8-app-on-gcp`.
6. **Step 6 — Enable Turbo Mode:** Say `"ciao"` to register the conversation in the left sidebar, and switch **Project Settings $\to$ Security Preset $\to$ Turbo mode** *(with explicit sandbox/non-prod safety disclaimer!)*.
7. **Step 7 — Prompt 2 (Start Guided Codelab):** Point Antigravity to the official Google Codelab (`#0`) and `workshop/landing-page/README.md`.
8. **Step 8 — Claim GCP Credits:** Redeem GCP trial credits on **Page 2 (`#1`)** of the Codelab using Google Chrome (verifying the green button).
9. **Step 9 — Prompt 3 (Automated GCP Project Setup):** Instruct Antigravity to create a timestamped project (`workshop-rails8-YYYYMMDD`), link the coupon billing account, and run `just workshop-test`.
